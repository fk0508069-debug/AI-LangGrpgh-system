FLOWLINE — COMPANY POLICY & AI AGENT SPECIFICATION
Public-Safe Version
No API keys, credentials, internal notes, or customer data included.

Note: Replace any [bracketed] items with final business decisions before publishing.
Internal infrastructure details are intentionally omitted from this public-safe file.

1. COMPANY & ACCESS
- Company type: E-commerce / D2C online store for workspace essentials
  (desks, lighting, seating, accessories).
- Who can use the system:
  - Customer
  - Admin
  - Employee/Agent (support, operations)
  - Vendor/Seller: No, unless Flowline later becomes a marketplace.
  - Other: None.
- Customer can see about their own account/order:
  - Profile details, order history, order status, tracking, invoices,
    shipping address, payment method last 4 digits, return/refund status.
- Must never be exposed to customers:
  - Admin data, internal notes, other customers' information, API keys,
    full payment card data, internal credentials, database access,
    employee-only logs, security configurations.

2. ACCOUNT POLICY
- Create account: Email + password; optional social login.
- Forgot password: Reset link sent to registered email; link expires.
- Change name/email/phone/address: Yes, from account settings.
  Email/phone may require verification.
- Suspected compromise: Account locked, forced password reset,
  user notified, activity reviewed.
- Multiple accounts: One personal account per email.
  Duplicate accounts for abuse are not allowed.

3. ORDER POLICY
- Place order: Through website checkout.
- Required info: Name, email, phone, shipping address, payment method, items.
- After order placed: Confirmation email, processing, shipping, tracking.
- Modify order: Yes, until status becomes "Processing" or "Shipped"
  depending on fulfillment.
- Modification stage: Before "Shipped"; while "Pending" or "Processing".
- Cancel order: Yes, before shipment.
- Cancellation stages: Pending / Processing.
- Already shipped: Cannot cancel; customer may request return after delivery.
- Wrong delivery address: Contact support immediately. If not shipped, update;
  if shipped, carrier may redirect or return to sender.

4. PAYMENT POLICY
- Payment methods: cash on delivery
  (as available at checkout).
- When charged: At checkout authorization/capture.
- Payment fails: Order is not placed; customer can retry.
- Charged but order fails: Automatic refund within 3–5 business days.
- Cash on Delivery: Not supported by default.
- Refunds: Yes, per return/refund policy.

5. SHIPPING & DELIVERY
- Locations: Supported countries/regions shown at checkout.
- Normal delivery time: 3–7 business days standard.
- Delayed delivery: Customer notified; support can investigate.
- Customer unavailable: Carrier may reattempt or leave notice.
- Lost package: File carrier claim; replace or refund if confirmed lost.
- Damaged package: Report within 48 hours with photos;
  replacement or refund.
- Track order: Yes, via email link and account dashboard.

6. RETURNS & REFUNDS
- Returns allowed: Yes.
- Return window: 30 days from delivery.
- Not eligible: Final sale, custom items, assembled/modified items,
  hygiene products, gift cards.
- Condition: Unused, original packaging, all parts/accessories.
- Return shipping cost: Customer pays unless item is defective or wrong.
- Request return: Through account or customer support.
- Approves/rejects: Returns team / support agent.
- Refund time: 5–10 business days after inspection.
- Refund sent: Original payment method.
- Damaged/used return: May be rejected or partially refunded.

7. PRODUCT POLICY
- Out of stock: Notify customer; offer restock alert;
  no backorder unless clearly stated.
- Preorders: Only if offered and clearly marked.
- Price changes after order: Price locked at order time.
- Wrong website price: Company may cancel and refund the order.
- Wrong product delivered: Replace or refund.
- Defective product: Return/replace per warranty and return policy.

8. CUSTOMER SUPPORT
- AI can handle: Order search, order status, tracking, product info,
  return policy, FAQ, create support ticket.
- Must transfer to human: Refunds, cancellations, account changes,
  complaints, legal issues, abuse, policy exceptions.
- AI should request before order discussion: Order ID and email.
- Verify identity before showing order info: Yes.
- AI cannot find order: Ask for correct details; escalate to human
  if still unresolved.
- Conflict with company policy: Explain policy; escalate if customer
  requests exception.

9. AI AGENT RULES
- Answer source: Only company policy/database.
  No general knowledge for policy decisions.
- No answer in policy: Say, "I don't have that information.
  I can connect you to a human agent."
- Make decisions: No. AI explains policy and performs only approved actions.
- Allowed actions:
  ✓ Search order
  ✓ Check order status
  ✓ Search products
  ✓ Explain return policy
  ✓ Create support ticket
- Not allowed:
  ✗ Cancel order
  ✗ Issue refund
  ✗ Change customer information
- Human approval required: Refunds, cancellations, account changes,
  policy exceptions.
- Conversation memory: Session memory allowed.
  Permanent memory only with consent.
- Retention: Conversation logs retained 90 days for quality/audit.
  Order data retained as required by law/accounting.

10. SECURITY & PRIVACY
- Access another person's order with order ID: No.
- Verification before order details: Order ID + email associated with order;
  additional verification if suspicious.
- Employee access levels: Yes, role-based access.
- Confidential customer information: PII, payment info, order history,
  internal notes, API keys, admin data.
- AI conversations logged: Yes, for auditing and quality.

11. ESCALATION
- Immediate human transfer: Legal threats, abuse, refund/cancellation
  requests, account compromise, policy exception, repeated AI failure.
- Abusive customer: Warn once; end conversation if abuse continues.
- Legal action: Escalate to human/legal team.
- Outside company policy: Explain policy; escalate if needed.
- Transfer message: "I'm connecting you to a human support agent
  who can help further."

12. AGENTIC AI DESIGN
- Agents wanted:
  Customer Support Agent
    ↓
  Order Agent
    ↓
  Product Agent
    ↓
  Return/Refund Agent
    ↓
  Policy/RAG Agent
    ↓
  Analytics Agent
    ↓
  Human Support
- One main AI or separate chatbots: One main orchestrator that delegates
  to specialized agents.
- AI actions: Limited actions only. No financial or account changes.
- Database: Internal only — not disclosed in public policy.
  Recommended internally: PostgreSQL + vector database for RAG.
- Framework: Internal only — recommended: LangGraph/LangChain + FastAPI.
- LLM: Internal only — configurable (OpenAI / Gemini / Groq / other).
- Final policy as RAG knowledge base: Yes. This document can be used
  as the public-safe knowledge base.

End of document.
