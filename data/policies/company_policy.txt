EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & COMPANY POLICY MANUAL
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 1.0
Department: Human Resources
1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
1.1 Welcome
Welcome to the team. This handbook has been prepared to introduce you to the policies, procedures,
and benefits that govern your employment with us. We are committed to providing a safe, productive,
and inclusive work environment.
1.2 At-Will Employment
Unless otherwise stated in a written contract, employment with the Company is "at-will." This means
that either you or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or
without cause or advance notice.
1.3 Policy Updates
The Company reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate any policy in this handbook at any
time. Nothing in this handbook creates a contract of employment.
2. CODE OF CONDUCT AND ETHICS
2.1 Professionalism
Employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally at all times. This includes treating
colleagues, clients, and vendors with courtesy and respect. Abusive language, shouting, or physical
intimidation is strictly prohibited.
2.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the
interests of the Company.
Disclosure: Any potential conflict of interest must be disclosed to a manager or HR immediately.
Outside Employment: Employees may engage in outside employment provided it does not interfere
with their duties or compete with the Company’s business.
2.3 Gifts and Bribery
Employees may not accept or offer bribes, kickbacks, or inappropriate gifts.
Acceptable Gifts: Token gifts of nominal value (under $50) may be accepted with manager approval.
Prohibited: Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards) are never acceptable.
2.4 Confidentiality
Employees have access to sensitive information including trade secrets, client lists, financial data, and
strategic plans.
Non-Disclosure: You may not disclose confidential information to outside parties during or after
employment.
Return of Property: All confidential materials and company property must be returned upon
termination.
2.5 Use of Company Assets
Company equipment (laptops, phones, vehicles) is provided for business purposes.
Personal Use: Limited personal use of computers and phones is permitted provided it does not incur
significant cost or reduce productivity.
No Expectation of Privacy: Employees should have no expectation of privacy regarding data stored on
company devices or communication sent via company email.
3. ATTENDANCE AND WORK SCHEDULES
3.1 Work Hours
Standard business hours are [Insert Hours, e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM], Monday through Friday. Specific
schedules may vary by department.
3.2 Punctuality
Employees are expected to be ready to work at their scheduled start time. Excessive tardiness or
absenteeism will result in disciplinary action.
3.3 Meal and Rest Breaks
Lunch: Employees working a full day are entitled to a [30/60]-minute unpaid meal break.
Rest: Two paid 15-minute rest breaks are provided per day.
3.4 Overtime
Non-exempt employees will receive overtime pay (1.5x regular rate) for hours worked over 40 in a
workweek. All overtime must be approved in advance by a supervisor. Unauthorized overtime is
prohibited.
3.5 Remote Work / Telecommuting
Remote work is a privilege, not a right, and is subject to performance and manager approval.
Availability: Remote employees must be reachable during core hours.
Workspace: Employees must maintain a safe and distraction-free home workspace.
4. LEAVE AND TIME OFF POLICIES
4.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
PTO is accrued based on years of service and is used for vacation, personal time, and sick leave.
Accrual: [Insert Accrual Rate, e.g., 1.25 days per month].
Requesting Time Off: Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance via the HR portal.
Carryover: A maximum of [Number] days may be carried over to the next calendar year.
4.2 Sick Leave
If you are unable to work due to illness, notify your manager as soon as possible, but no later than
one hour before your shift starts.
4.3 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)
Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for specific family
and medical reasons. (Refer to the HR department for detailed eligibility requirements).
4.4 Bereavement Leave
In the event of the death of an immediate family member, employees may take up to [3-5] days of
paid leave.
4.5 Jury Duty
Employees summoned for jury duty will be granted unpaid leave (or paid leave if state law requires).
You must provide a copy of the summons.
5. WORKPLACE SAFETY AND SECURITY
5.1 Zero Tolerance for Violence
The Company prohibits threats, threatening behavior, or acts of violence by anyone on company
premises. Weapons are strictly forbidden on company property.
5.2 Drug and Alcohol Policy
Prohibited Substances: The use, possession, or sale of illegal drugs on company property is grounds
for immediate termination.
Alcohol: Consuming alcohol during work hours (excluding approved company events) is prohibited.
Impairment: Reporting to work under the influence of any substance that impairs performance is
prohibited.
5.3 Workplace Hygiene
Employees are expected to maintain a clean and organized workspace. Kitchens and common areas
must be kept tidy.
5.4 Emergency Procedures
In the event of a fire, medical emergency, or natural disaster, employees must follow the instructions
of the designated Safety Warden. Evacuation routes are posted in all hallways.
6. ANTI-HARASSMENT AND NON-DISCRIMINATION
6.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
The Company is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion,
sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or genetic information.
6.2 Harassment Defined
Harassment includes unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics. This includes:
Verbal: Slurs, jokes, or offensive comments.
Physical: Assault, unwanted touching, or blocking movement.
Visual: Offensive posters, cartoons, or emails.
Quid Pro Quo: Submission to such conduct is made a term or condition of employment.
6.3 Reporting Procedures
If you experience or witness harassment:
Report it immediately to your supervisor or HR.
If the supervisor is the subject of the complaint, report directly to HR or the CEO.
All complaints will be investigated promptly and confidentially.
6.4 Retaliation
Retaliation against an employee for reporting harassment or participating in an investigation is strictly
prohibited and will result in termination.
